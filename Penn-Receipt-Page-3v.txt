To Make Quinch marmelet
take Cool’d Quinces & pulp ye through a Sieve and weigh ye and take their weight in Sugar & make it in a Syrup with a little water and yen put in ye pulps and boyle it Clear & colour it with Saffron & boyle all till it be stiff and Clear yen put it in pots

To Make Lemon Drops

Take a pound of Sifted Sugar put in a Lemon Rinds grated fine and a little piece as much Froth of Egg as will make a stiff paste and beat it very white & mix colours to your fancy & drop ye upon white paper and baket ye in a Cool Oven

To Make Marble drg

Take half a pound of doble Refind Sugar and 1/4 of fine mix to it a little Gumdrogon Steeped to a jelly and as much froth of Egg as will make it light Enough & polis to it a little juces of Orange or rosewater and Orange flower water & beat it very white & ye drop it in little drops and Mix Colours to fancy and drie them by the fire

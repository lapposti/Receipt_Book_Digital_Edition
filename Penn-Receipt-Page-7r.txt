To Preserve Strawberry's
Take the full ripe and Crush them and put a pound of Sugar to a pint and 1/2 and a little water to the best part of the Sugar and make it into a Clear Syrup and when could put the Strawberrys in and boyle them 2 and Scum them and put them of the fire 2 or 3 times and Strow Sugar over them 2 or 3 times by a while out of the fire. Let them over again and boyle them Clear and the Syrup thick so Glas them up

To Dry Pears
take Lording's or bob pears and Pear them & put them in a jug & as much Spring water as will cover them 2 and may put to 20 pears 2 pound of Sugar and 1/2 penny worth of Sutcheanel and tye them away and put it in the Syrup with the pears Steep them up and let them Stand in an oven that his Cooks all Night & take them out and lay them to dry on Sives or dishes and Set them in a slow and Cool place to dry and turn them as you See occation till the be dry 3...

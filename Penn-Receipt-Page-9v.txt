To Pickle Samphers
Clean it well from dirt and cut of the root and put it up in old Salt, beef and Salt in a clean Scoured Pan and let it heat Safely till it be Green and when you See it a good Green & lay it out to dry and boyle as much Allegar and Spices as will cover it when Could & put it out and keep it for Use

To Pickle Onions
take Small onions and Scald them a little and pill them put them in Salt and water and let them Stand all Night and heat them and dry them on a Cloth and put them in bottles and a little mace & peper and cover them with distill’d vinegar and cork them up and blether them

To Pickle Oysters
open them Carefully for losing the pickle and Scald them in their own lickquor and if clean them from Shells & let the lickquor Settle and power it into a Clean Saucepan and put to it a little mace white peper Salt and a little white wine Vinegar boyle...

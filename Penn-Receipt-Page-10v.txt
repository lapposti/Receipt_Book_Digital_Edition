(Continuation of To Pickle Mushrooms)
boyle them and drain them & dry them then ginger and all sorts of Spieces put them in wide mouthed bottles and cover them with fine Vinegar a little mace peper Salt cork and blether them up from the air

To Make Cattchup
take large mushrooms and pill them and bruise them and Sprinkle Salt over them and let it Stand all Night and then boyle it and Strain it through a Sive and let it Settle and boyle and Scum them and put a little nutmey mace cloves and whole peper and a little Anchoves and Shallot a trace of ginger and boyle 3 or 4 times while & put it by when you have boyled it till it does well taste of the Seasoning and Clear it into Clean pots and put a little mace and Nutmey in every bottle

To Pickle Sage mint or Parsley
take the herbs fresh grown and put them in Salt and water & let them Stand till yellow & wash them Clear out and then put them in a Clean Scoured Pan with water...

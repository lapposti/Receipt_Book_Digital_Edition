(Continuation of To Make Ratefee biscaket)
...put in an Ounce of bitter Almonds & mix Froth of Egg and make them up in a paste and Cut them in little bits of biscaket a Shilling and baket them on writing paper laid in a slow Oven

To Make Sugar Puffs
Take a pound of Sugar Sifted & mix in an ounce of Jordon almonds beaten fine & a little gum & gold some froth of Egg & make it into a past and take a little of the past & Mix it every Colour all of an equal thickness and mix the Colours to your fancy and put them through a Squid upon wafer or paper and baket them in a slow oven

To Make lemon wafers and twills
Take a pound of doble Refind Sugar Sifted fine & put to it as much gumdrogon Steeped in juces of lemon as will make it a stiff past mix it with a Clean Knife & put it upon a tray to keep moist & mix a little Colours to your past and then mix it up together & your fancy & Role them out even & thin and Dry them upon Rols to prepare to make twills mix a little colour & a little white & Role them in lengths & tize them to your fancy as if some past you may make toys if you have moulds

To Make white Corran jelly
take Clear white Corrans Strip them & put them in a jug put in a spoon of water & boyle them...

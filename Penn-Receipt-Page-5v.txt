To Make bulloc Cheeße
Take 5 or 6 quarts of bullocs & put the in a jug and Set them in an oven till the pulp them through a Sive and take better the their weight in Sugar let the Sugar be very hard dryed and the put in the pulps in a pan boyle it a good while & Stiff and put in of Sugar very hot let boyle very Stiff the put it in Tonken pots & when could paper it up

To Make red Aple paste
take Clear ripes aples codlins or pepins & coole them in a thin Skin & take the pulps of the inside and put it through a Sive and weigh it & to half and to half a pound of pulps & ounces of loaf Sugar boyle the pulps by it Self with a little Saffron & a little water when you have boyle’d it pretty high put it to the Sugar you must wet your Sugar with water & boyle it to a Candy & put in the pulp boyle it till all the Sugar be you Clear in the pulp when you see it thicken take it of the fire & put it on past glas and set fine Stock...

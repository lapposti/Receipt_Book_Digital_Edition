To Pickle Cockles or Mussels
Take the lickquor and Scald the fish in it and drain them well out and put in a little mace peper Salt and when you have boyled and Scum it Clear put in a little of the Vinegar and put it on the fish So keep them for Use

To Pickle Salmon
when you have clean’d it & cut it to your mind Salt it an hour or 2 and Season all the inside very well with mace peper Salt and a few Cloves a little sweet orangerine & bake it well as you do Sturgeon and put it upon a fish plate and put it in a broad pan with Vinegar and water Salt and Spices when Enough Strain it well and Set the pickle to Settle when could put the Salmon in a mug or cask and power the lickquor on the top and power on a little Sweet Oyle

To Pickle Mushrooms

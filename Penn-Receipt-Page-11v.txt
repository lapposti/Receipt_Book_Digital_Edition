To Pot woodcocks or partridge
take the Clean cocks if you get it and draw them and wipe out all the blood and then Season them with black pepar Salt and Nutmey and mace and Seure them up with the bills and put butter in every one of the bellies put them with the breast downward in a mug and bake them tender and then drain the butter and Gravey out

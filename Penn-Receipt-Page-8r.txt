To make chocolate drops
take half a pound of Sifted Sugar & 2 ounces of fine Chocolate grated a little gum Dragon and as much whites of Egg as will make it Soft Enough to drop. drop them on wafer paper and bake them in a Slow oven

To Make whip Silibubs
take a quart of Cream and mix a little Sugar a peece, & Lemon pill and a little wisck which doe whip froth and lay it on Sives to drain all night fill the Glass above half full of white wine & Claret and then keep up the froth as high as you Can

To make Orange Butter
take the whites of 6 Eggs boyled hard put in a pound of butter & a little Sugar with a Spoonful of orange Flower water work it through a Sive on Plates

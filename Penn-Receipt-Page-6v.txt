To make plumb Paste
take Green Plumbs codle & pill them & gree them in the same water and pulp them & mix a little aple to it boyle it well by it Self and put in Sugar being very hot & dry to thicken it over the fire till the Sugar be Clear out and Glas it up

To Make Rasberry Paste
Drouse a pint of Raspes the Seeds fall boyle them by them Selves till the be pretty dry & put in their doble weight of Sugar very hot and dry & heat it over the fire till the Clear so glas it up and put them in Stoves and when hard of one Side turn them on Glas till the be hard Enough to Box

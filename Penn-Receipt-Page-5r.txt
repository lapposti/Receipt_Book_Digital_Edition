To Make Goosberry marma (Continuation)
Take Goosberrys and Codles & pulp ye through a Sive and then a few Green Plumbs and weigh ye pulps and take their weight in Sugar & boyle ye pulps and Sugar mix in the Sugar & thicken it over a Slow fire till all the Sugar be disolved and it thick so put it in Marmalede pots and keep it for Use

To Make Corran Marmelet for jelly
when you have run the juce through a pulp of Corrans through a hair Sive & take their weight in Sugar & boyle & Scum the pulps very well and drie your Sugar very hot & put it in & boyle it till you see it jelly so put it in little pots when Cold paper it up

To Make Marmelet of green Codlins
Take a pound of Codle and Green & peel’d & Scrape Said fall the green out Side & pulp it through a Sive & take its weight in Sugar & put a little water to boyle & Scum it & put in the pulps boyle it up Clear & Stiff so put it in pots when Cold paper it up

To Make while Rasberry marmelet
Take 2 quarts of white Rasberrys and 2 quarts of ripe corrans and pulp them both through a Sive & weigh the pulps take their weight in loaf Sugar Sifted & dry'd hot boyle and Scum the pulps a good while by it Self and & put in the Sugar & boyle & Scum it till it be a jelly So put it in little pots & Glasses when Cold paper it up

To Pickle Cabage
take deep coloured red Cabage cut out the Stalks & Shred the Cabage & Season it with peper Salt ginger and a little mace let it lye an hour and put it Close down in a jug & power on as much clear Allegar as will cover it and tye it close up with a blather

To Pickle Collyflowers
take whitest collyflowers cut of branches as large as you please into Salt water & put them in a pan with boyling water & a little Salt and just give them a boyle & take them up and dry them on a Cloth and put them in a wide bottle and put Vinegar and let it lye 3 or 4 days & power that of and Season it with mace and peper & powder of fresh pickle and Cork and blether it...

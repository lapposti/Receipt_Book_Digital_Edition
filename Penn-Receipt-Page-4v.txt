To Preserve Green Codlins
Take 1/2 of a midlin Size fresh of Silver Rind & in cold water cover the Codlins & Scrape with ye back pill & ye pill put ye in of water again cover up with ye rinds and Scrape and heat ye Softly till ye be green and tender but take Care of yeir bursting & drain ye from ye water without ye flower end & Stick a penknife to ye Cork and ye put to ye as much thick Syrup as will cover ye you must mix a pint of water to a pound of Sugar boyle it Clear & when Cold put ye Codlins in Stir ye softly 2 or 3 Days till ye be taken of ye Syrup & drain ye out & boyle ye whole in a thicker Syrup and put in a drop or 2 of Ginger Spieces of lemon rine when you see ye Clear & ye Syrup thick put ye in at ye Mouth of a jug & bleyer ye up

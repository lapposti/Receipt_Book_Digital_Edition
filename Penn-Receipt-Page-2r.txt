To Preserve Rosberys
Take ye before they are ripes and weight in doble refind Sugar & put a little water to ye better half of your Sugar and make it into a Syrup put in ye Rosberys & boyle & Scum it Clear and then put in of drie Sugar to thicken up the Syrup Scum it Clear and Glas ye up.

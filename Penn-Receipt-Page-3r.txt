to Preserve Green plumbs or Green Aprecocks

take ye when a spin will go through ye and put ye in Scalding water till ye will change and ye rest of ye half ye fill & put ye in a 1/2 pint of water again and Cover ye and green ye and put to ye yeir weight in doble refind Sugar and a little water boyle and Scum ye Clear So put ye in Glasses or Jotts if you put ye in Glasses you must put ye in anoyer Syrup

To Make Orange Marmelet

Boyle oranges as you do for preserving and cut ye very thin and take yeir weight in Sugar & put a little water to it boyle it Clear & put in ye rinds & ye juces Strain'd Clear boyle all till it will jelly & Glas it

To Make Corrant Jelly

Take fresh Corrans & strip ye and Set ye over ye fire in a pott or in a pan of water many of juces and mash ye Corrans to apiece and put 2 pound of Sugar doble refind boyle and Scum it Clear & put a little of ye water where ye Sugar melt & boyle it Clear yen put in ye juces & boyle & Scum till it will jelly yen Glass it up

To Make Apple Past
Take Apples Codled in like manner as the other and pulp them, weigh them then add to the pulp, a little Corran juce disolved, and a little fine Refined Smelt boyle it to a good green Land Stir it to your jug. as before when Dry turn your pan on Sheets of Glas

To make corran Paste
Pulps Corrans through a Hair Sive and weigh the pulps, put to one the a pound of Sugar, then beat the Sugar very fine. Dry it very hot and boyle the pulps by it self very well. then add in the Sugar Stir it very well over a Slow fire untill it thicken then put it in your Stove to dry

To make goosbury Paste
Take green goosburries before they are ripe Codle them. & pulp them through a Sive and weigh the pulps & put the double Quantity of Sugar. & Set the Sugar Dry & very hot. Then boyle the pulp, mix your Sugar to it Stirring it till it thicken. & take care you do not boil it too, power it...

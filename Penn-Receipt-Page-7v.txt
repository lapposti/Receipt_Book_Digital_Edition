To Dry Plumbs
take large red or white plumbs & take half their weight in Sugar make a Syrup with a little water when Could put in the plumbs being wiped and pricked and heat them a little at a time till the be past breaking & boyle & Scum them a little till you have taken of the Syrup set them by for 4 or 5 Days in the Syrups then lay them out to dry and turn them as the dry laster they are dryed past Stickin of both Sides you may run them through a thin Candy

To Dry Corrans any Bunch
take large bunches of Corrans and pick out the Seeds carefully and Strow Sugar over them and make a Syrup to boyle with 1/2 of loaf Sugar and water and when hot could put in the Corrans and boyle it a little and let them look in the Syrup till you think they are taken with the Syrups lay them out to Dry and tye good bunches & box them

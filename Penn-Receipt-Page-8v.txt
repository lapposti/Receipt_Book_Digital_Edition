To make Sack Papist
take 14 Eggs leaues out the half of whites beat them up with a quarter of a pound of white Sugar, a quarter of a pint of Sack mix all well together and Set it on the fire and keep Stirring all one way when Scalding put in another white and Stir it power into the aquart of Cream boyling hot with a matter put one boyled in & take it of the fire and put it on pipe plates on and let it Stain a quarter of an hour

To pickle Cucumbers
Take midling cucumbers & put them in a Strong Salt & water and let them Stand a week and then boyle Salt and water & put on them often till you see them a good Green & boyle good Salt allegar & Set it Steep and put in Seasoning to your mind and power it upon them and when Cold put on a little kill and blether it up

To pickle kidney beans
take midling Size and put them up in Salt and water and when yellow wash them Clean out and put them in a clean Pan...

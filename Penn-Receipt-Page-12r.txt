(Continuation of To Pot a hare)
yen drain ye gravey out and chop it small and as you chop it put in Clarifyed butter & and ye put it close down in a pot and cover it with butter you must put ye butter amount what it baked with and a little fat of boyled bacon and Set it where it will not shake

To Make fine pancakes of milk
take a pint of milk & eight Eggs whole nutmey grated & a little Salt & melt a pound of butter & a little Sack & before you Stir it it must be as thick with flower as ordinary batter and fry it with larde and turn it ye back Side of a plate & garnish with orange & Sugar

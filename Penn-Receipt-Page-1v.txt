To Preserve Morrab Cherrys
Take one pound and half of Cherres half a pint of Corran juces Stone the Cherries and put the end of juces with 2-1/2 pound of knock’d Sugar all in a pan together & put it above over and boyle and Scum the Clear till the Syrup be thick So put them in Mugs or Glasses

To Preserve Gooseberyes
Pick them and put them into a Clean Pan and take their weight in Sugar make the better half into Syrup with a little Corran juces boyle them a little you must not Boyles yem but do yem softly when you have well taken off the Syrup boyle & Scum them and put in the rest of the Sugar and when they begin to jelly put them in mugs or Glasses

Continuation of To Pickle Sage mint or Parsley
cover them close & put them over a Clear fire when hot take them of continue Doing So till it be a good green and ye Shift them into another hot water and put them over again when a Clear Green dry them on a Cloth and boyle good Allegar and put it on ye when it is cold after the Same mayer you may pickle pears sweets

To Pickle herrings
take them fresh got clean’d and Salt them a little and wash them and wipe them Season the insides with nutmey peper Salt sweet margerom and put them close down in a mug and them cover it with Vinegar & water and lay fennels all over and tye it up & Stand 3 quarters of an hour in the oven So take them out of the pickle & put them with vinegar and oyle

To Pot Beeff
take 6 pound of bullock Beef and Skin it and Slash it and Season it with black peper and let it lye 2 nights and Strow on of peper and Salt nutmey and cover it with butter and let it Stand in the oven all night...

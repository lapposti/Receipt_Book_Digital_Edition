(Continuation of ye previous recipe, likely Jampans)
take ye if not too ripe ye weight in good Powder Sugar & put ye better half in a roasting Pan with a little water to melt it boyle it Clear & wipe ye Neck of ye dampt Pan and put ye in and heat ye a little if set up by and Strow Some Sugar above ye head 2 or 3 times till ye be past breaking put in ye rest of ye Sugar boyle and Scum Clear and So put ye in Mugs or Glasses

To Preserve white Corrans
take large white bunches & pick out all ye seeds yen dust fine doble refind Sugar over as you do ye and it takes Corran juces at 1/2 pound of Sugar and half a pint of water boyle it Clear give ye Corrans a Scald in a little of ye Syrup & put ye in ye Glasses & put half a pint or a little more of ye juces to Syrup boyle and & Scum it till it jelly So put it upon ye Corrans in ye Glass

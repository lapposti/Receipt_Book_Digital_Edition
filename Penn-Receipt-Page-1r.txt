To prepare Oringes

Take fresh oringes lay them to Steep in water two Days then take out the inmeat and put them in boyling water & boyle them Tender and Scrape out the inside then put them in a syne made of Loaf Sugar if put to one pound of Sugar half a pint of water boyle them Clear when cold put the oringes in & boyle them to or three Days a little at a time in a pott. when the Syrup draw'd off out & put the in a fresh Syrup and keep them for Use

To Candy Lemons and oringes
Preserve yet as if other in a first Syrup let yem be a good while in the Syrup about 2 weeks then lay yem out to dry when dry dip Loaf Sugar in water boyle it then it and when it begins to Candy put your lemons in and let them be heated throughly then lay them out to Dry
